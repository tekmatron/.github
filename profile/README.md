# Welcome to TEKMATRON

**Physical traceability. No manual scanning.**  
*Trazabilidad física. Sin escaneos manuales.*

TEKMATRON is a company working at the intersection of industrial technology, automated logistics, and physical traceability. We are interested in a simple question: how can organizations understand what is happening to physical objects as they move through real operations?

We bring together people who think about the physical environment, the systems that observe it, and the software that helps teams act on what they learn. 

## What guides us

- **Start with the real workflow.** Technology should fit the conditions people and objects actually encounter in warehouses and industrial environments.
- **Make the invisible understandable.** Operational events should be clear, attributable, and useful to the people responsible for acting on them.
- **Build with care.** Reliability, maintainability, safety, and clear failure behavior matter as much as a successful demonstration.
- **Integration with context awareness.** Every organization has existing systems and constraints. Good technology should connect to them thoughtfully.
- **Evidence based.** We distinguish prototypes, validated capabilities, and production commitments. We do not treat an experiment as a promise.
- **Keep learning together.** We value careful questions, documented decisions, and respectful disagreement.

## How we collaborate

We welcome constructive conversations with engineers, industrial operators, researchers, integration partners, and others interested in traceability and logistics technology.

- Read the repository’s own README before using or contributing to a project.
- For substantial changes or proposed collaborations, open a discussion or issue first so we can align on the need and context.
- Share enough information to make a proposal understandable, but do not post credentials, private customer data, or confidential operational details.
- Keep feedback respectful, specific, and focused on the work.
- Treat prototypes as prototypes. Confirm a project’s status and limitations before relying on it in an operational setting.

## Trust, safety, and responsible use

**Security reports:** Please do not publish exploitable vulnerability details or sensitive customer information in a public issue. Use GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting for the relevant repository when available. If it is not enabled, contact the organization through an established private channel.

**Data and privacy:** Do not upload customer records, personal data, facility-sensitive information, production logs, credentials, or keys unless a project explicitly documents an approved and protected process. Use synthetic or anonymized examples for public discussion.

**Respectful community:** We expect participation to be professional and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and retaliation are not acceptable. Raise concerns privately with the maintainers of the relevant repository.

**Use and licensing:** Permissions are defined by the license and notices in each repository. This organization-wide page does not grant a blanket license to reuse code, designs, documentation, or brand assets. If a repository has no license, ask its maintainers before reusing its contents.

## Working with a repository

Projects in this organization may have different maturity levels, owners, and contribution rules. Before relying on or contributing to one, check its README, license, security policy, and recent activity. When contributing, explain the problem you are addressing and include relevant context or verification. Maintainers may ask for changes, defer a proposal, or decline contributions that do not fit a project’s scope.

## Get in touch

For general questions, partnerships, or collaboration proposals, contact the TEKMATRON team through the contact channel listed on our company website or in the relevant project repository. For security concerns, follow the private reporting guidance above.
